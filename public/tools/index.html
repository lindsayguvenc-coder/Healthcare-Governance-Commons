Community Weaving Tools
Interactive Tools

Governance infrastructure
you can actually use

Frameworks are only as good as what they help you do. These tools are the Commons made operational — AI-powered, grounded in the governance architecture, built for practitioners working in real conditions.

◎
No account required, and the Commons doesn't save what you type. Some tools send your answers to an AI model to write parts of the result, and each of those says so; leave out patient details. The Local Validation Kit and Agent QC Setup run entirely in your browser. Each tool is built on the governance frameworks documented in the Commons and the Community Weaving series — they are the infrastructure, made interactive.
Tools
01
AI-Powered · Good Containers
The Disagreement Unpacker

Disagreement can be information about the system, not just a problem to resolve. Describe a real disagreement and the tool walks through nine lenses: facts, assumptions, values, lived experience, incentives, risks, time horizons, definitions, and who carries the cost if it goes wrong. If your description doesn't cover something, it says so.

Live ↗
02
AI-Powered · Good Containers
Container Health Check

Describe your governance container and the tool reads it against eight properties, including who can stop a tool, whether the group can say no, and whether it sees what AI is really in use. It gives no score: it shows what you said, what's missing, and what to ask your group.

Live ↗
03
AI-Powered · Good Containers
The Charter Starter

Answer five questions and get a first draft charter to argue with: six principles as rules, metrics, and failsafes, plus who can stop a tool and what happens when a clinician and the AI disagree. A conversation starter, not a template.

Live ↗
04
Rule-Rated · Six Value Areas · AI-Written Explanation
ROI Assessment

Value from clinical AI is claimed broadly and measured rarely. This tool rates how strong the evidence is across six areas — clinical outcomes, operational impact, financial return (net of the cost of checking the tool), equity and harm, community value, and the non-monetary currencies governance rarely tracks — and sorts what you know into measured, assumed and unknown. Ratings come from fixed rules; AI writes the explanation. Built for governance committees deciding whether to continue or sunset, procurement teams evaluating adoption, and physician champions who need to build the case. Recommendations come only when your AI strategy is documented and in use.

Live ↗
05
Rule-Based · Adaptive Elicitation · PDF Output
Validation Protocol Generator

Keep your model card and validation study handy. A structured 6-section adaptive elicitation maps your organization, patient population, validation approach, contingency design, monitoring infrastructure, and challenge program to a complete, institution-specific validation protocol. Full quantitative bias assessment included. Built on the Commons governance architecture.

Live ↗
06
Runs in Your Browser · Rules First · Short Review Queue
Local Validation Kit

Test a clinical AI model on your own past patients. Rules label most encounters automatically; a person reviews only the cases the rules can't decide, plus a blind sample that checks the rules. Out come the four counts, intervals and per-group numbers the Validation Protocol needs. Your file never leaves your browser. First rule pack: sepsis (CDC Adult Sepsis Event). Paused until a clinician signs off the rules and a statistician checks the math.

Paused · In review ↗
07
Guided Setup · Lab QC for AI Agents · Prototype
Agent QC Setup

The nine kinds of quality control a laboratory runs, mapped to a clinical AI agent. Every set point has a starting value and a plain statement of what the value means in clinical operations, so a team that changes nothing still has a defensible plan. Includes the former Metric Picker's statistics, lanes set per control, and a QC plan to confirm at validation. Fills in the values the Validation Protocol Generator leaves "set at validation."

Prototype ↗
08
Directory · Methodology, Not Data
Peer Challenge Registry

A directory of how organizations running the same AI systems built their challenge sets and blind-run protocols, so peers can learn from methodology without sharing patient data or performance numbers. A preview of what it would look like: every entry is a made-up demo, and registration is paused while the Commons is a one-person prototype.

Preview · Paused ↗
09
Reference · 57 Sourced Clauses · Lives Outside the Commons
Feed It to the Beast: Procurement

Enterprise AI procurement the way labs already survive(ish) it. Contract clauses for buying clinical AI, each traced to what binding law already requires of vendors in labs, blood supply, pharma, aviation, banking and other regulated fields: audit rights, disaggregated performance data, notice before updates, the right to suspend. Checked clause by clause against its sources.

Live · Opens another site ↗

You know your own questions; this is the other side of the table.
